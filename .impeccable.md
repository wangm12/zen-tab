## Design Context

### Users
Heavy Chrome tab users managing several parallel work projects. They use Zen Tab during active research, project switching, and end-of-day cleanup. The job is to make a crowded browser feel legible without interrupting flow.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, quietly opinionated. The interface should evoke control and relief, not urgency or novelty.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, editorial utility panel with warm ink surfaces, a restrained chartreuse signal color, and small amber status notes. Dense enough for 300 tabs, but with deliberate rhythm and strong text hierarchy. Avoid generic dashboard cards, gradients, and decorative noise.

### Design Principles
- Make the next useful action obvious without shouting.
- Treat every automated action as reversible and explainable.
- Use motion only to clarify state changes.
- Keep visual density high while preserving clear hit targets.
- Let uncertainty be visible instead of hiding it.
